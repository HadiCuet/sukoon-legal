# Sukoon — Support

Sukoon plays the Quran over calm night sounds so you can fall asleep to it.

## Common questions

### Does Sukoon know when Fajr is?
No, and it never claims to. The alarm is **a time you set yourself**. Sukoon does not ask for your location and does not compute prayer times.

### What is free?
Every reciter, every surah, every playlist and the alarm — for ever. You can keep three nights without a subscription, built by you or taken from the library.

### What does Sukoon Pro add?
Every place to sleep in, your own photograph as a place, extra sounds in the levels, whole reciters downloaded, and as many nights as you like.

### A night stopped playing after my subscription ended
A night whose **room** is part of Pro does not start again once a subscription ends. The recitation itself is never withheld: build a night in the free place, or in any place you already have, and it plays for ever.

### Where does the recitation come from?
From the public archives that host it — everyayah.com and Quran.com. The reciters, sounds and photographs are credited in the app, under Settings → **Sounds and pictures**.

### I subscribed on another iPhone or iPad
Open Settings → **Restore a purchase**, signed in to the same Apple Account. The paywall has the same button.

### How do I cancel Sukoon Pro?
In your Apple Account settings: open the Settings app, tap your name, then **Subscriptions**. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends and you are not charged again. Every night built from free places keeps playing.

### The recitation stops partway through
A night plays from files fetched as it goes. If the connection drops, playback waits and then carries on. Download the night in advance to avoid it entirely.

## Still stuck?

Write to **mukashi.dev@gmail.com** with your device, your iOS version and what happened. Replies usually come within two days.
