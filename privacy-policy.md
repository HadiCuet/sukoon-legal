# Sukoon — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 2, 2026

Sukoon plays the Quran over calm night sounds so you can fall asleep to it. It is built to be private: there is no account, no advertising and no tracking. This policy explains the little data the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

## 1. Who we are

Sukoon is developed and operated by **Abdullah Al Hadi** ("we", "us"). For the EU / UK / EEA General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), Abdullah Al Hadi is the **data controller** for personal data processed through the app. For any privacy question, write to **mukashi.dev@gmail.com**.

## 2. Data the app handles

### Stays only on your device

Everything you make in Sukoon stays on your device. If you use iCloud Backup, Apple includes it in your encrypted backup; that is between you and Apple. None of it is sent to us.

- **Your nights:** the list you build — what is recited, the reciter, the place, the sound levels and the names you give them.
- **Settings:** which screen the app opens on, your default reciter, the wake time, and the sleep timer you last chose.
- **Downloaded recitation:** the audio files you choose to download, kept so a night plays without a connection. You can remove them in Settings.
- **Your photograph:** if you make a place from a picture of your own, you choose it with Apple's photo picker. Sukoon receives only that one picture, keeps a copy inside the app, and never sees the rest of your library.

### Leaves your device

- **Recitation audio.** When you play or download a recitation, the app requests the audio files directly from the public archives that host them — **everyayah.com** and **api.qurancdn.com** (Quran.com). As with opening any web address, those services receive your device's IP address and the file requested. They are not operated by us, and we receive nothing from them. Their own policies apply to what they log.
- **Subscriptions.** If you subscribe to Sukoon Pro, **Apple** handles the payment and tells the app only whether a subscription is active. We never see your name, your card or your Apple Account. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase.

### Not collected

Sukoon has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party trackers. It does not ask for your location, your contacts, your microphone or access to your photo library. It does not know when Fajr is anywhere, because it never asks where you are — the wake time is a time you set yourself.

## 3. Why we handle it

The data above exists so the app can do what you asked: keep the nights you made, play the recitation you chose, and wake you when you said. Under the GDPR the lawful basis is **performance of a contract** (giving you the app you installed) and, for a subscription, **a legal obligation** on Apple to record the purchase.

## 4. Children

Sukoon is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. The app is usable by anyone and collects no personal data from any user.

## 5. How long it is kept

Everything the app stores stays on your device until you delete it or delete the app. We hold no copy. Deleting Sukoon removes your nights, your settings, your photograph and every downloaded file.

## 6. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or erase on your behalf. You can see, change or remove everything in the app itself, and remove all of it by deleting the app. For a purchase, write to Apple. If you are in the EU / UK / EEA you have the right to complain to your data protection authority; in California, the CCPA/CPRA rights of access, deletion and non-discrimination apply, and we do not sell or share personal information.

## 7. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it and the new version is published at this address.
